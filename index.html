IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606) Notes

Free · Open source · Bilingual (中文 / English) · Aligned with the Cambridge 0606 syllabus

A complete, free and open-source set of notes for Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606), reorganised around the official syllabus rather than the coursebook. Every chapter includes full derivations, step-by-step worked solutions and the common pitfalls that cost marks in the exam. Available in Chinese and English.

这是一套面向 剑桥 IGCSE 附加数学（0606） 的完整笔记：按考纲而不是教材目录重新组织， 包含完整推导、逐步解答与常见易错点，永久免费、开源、无付费墙。
